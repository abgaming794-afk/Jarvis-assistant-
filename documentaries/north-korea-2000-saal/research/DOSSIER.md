## Angle (why ours is different)
Ziro History (534x) and Hidden History 777 (118x) already did "2000 saal" as a straight timeline. Our thread: **one city, Pyongyang, and one proverb**, "Jab whales ladti hain, jheenge ki peeth tootti hai" (고래 싸움에 새우등 터진다). Every era is a big power invading the peninsula. That 2000-year fear explains today's North Korea: Juche, isolation, nukes. The payoff: the "shrimp" grew spines (became a porcupine).

## A1. Myth and Gojoseon

| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Dangun myth | Legendary founder of Gojoseon, traditional date **2333 BCE**. Myth, not history. | New World Encyclopedia "Dangun"; Reuters quote (archaeologist Lee Chung Kyu: "Dangun is a myth") |
| NK "tomb" claim | **Oct 1993**: NK announced 86 bones of Dangun and his wife dated "5,011 years"; mausoleum built near Pyongyang (1994). Kim Il Sung insisted Dangun was real. | Wikipedia "Mausoleum of Tangun"; Malay Mail/Reuters (2018) |

| Lelang | Chinese commandery, seat near **Pyongyang**, **108 BCE – 313 CE** (≈400 years) | same. Some historians dispute the location (**Disputed**, mention lightly) |
| NK's own name | DPRK calls itself **Chosŏn** (조선), from Gojoseon/Joseon; South calls itself Hanguk | standard |

## 8. Misconceptions to bust
1. "Kim Jong Il Paektu pahad pe paida hue" → Soviet records say USSR, 1941. (Disputed — show both)
2. "Viral video me Kim ki maut ka announcement" → it was the 2011 Kim Jong Il broadcast.
3. "Kim Ju Ae officially waaris ghoshit" → Only South Korean intelligence says so; Pyongyang has said nothing.
4. "North Korea communist desh hai, to raaj family ka nahi" → Three generations of one family; constitution centres the "Paektu bloodline" (state term).
