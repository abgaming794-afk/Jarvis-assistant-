## CHAPTER 1: Ek bhaalu, ek devta aur pehla Chinese raaj

### S05 | FOOTAGE | AI illustration: a bear in a cave eating garlic, soft ink style (label "AI-generated illustration")
Korea ki kahani ek myth se shuru hoti hai. Ek bhaalu gufa mein band raha, sirf lahsun aur mugwort kha kar, aur aurat ban gayi. Uski shaadi aasmaan ke devta ke bete se hui. Unka beta tha Dangun. Myth ke hisaab se Dangun ne 2333 BCE mein pehla Korean rajya banaya, Gojoseon.

### S06 | DOCUMENT | 1993 excavation report (illustration) with "5,011 saal" circled; stamp "DISPUTED"
Ye ek kahani hai. Lekin 1993 mein North Korea ne ailaan kiya ki use Dangun ki asli haddiyan mil gayi hain, paanch hazaar saal purani. Pyongyang ke paas ek bada mausoleum bana diya gaya. Kim Il Sung kehte the ki Dangun koi myth nahi, asli insaan the.

### S07 | TEXT | split card: "North Korea: Dangun ki kabr" vs "Bahar ke historians: Goguryeo daur ki kabr"
Bahar ke historians is daave ko nahi maante. North Korea ne kisi independent jaanch ki ijaazat nahi di. South Korean historians ke mutabik ye kabr shayad bahut baad ke Goguryeo daur ki hai. Lekin sawaal ye hai ki ek desh ko paanch hazaar saal purane devta ki zaroorat kyun padi? Is sawaal pe hum wapas aayenge.

### S08 | MAP | Han China expands east; arrow into the north of the peninsula; four commandery labels appear; pin "Lelang" near Pyongyang
Ab asli itihaas. 108 BCE mein China ke Han samrajya ne Gojoseon ko hara diya. Uttar Korea mein chaar Chinese prashasanik ilaake bane. Inme sabse bada tha Lelang, jiska kendra aaj ke Pyongyang ke paas tha.

### S09 | STAT-COUNTER | "108 BCE → 313 CE" bar fills; label "≈ 400 saal"
Lagbhag chaar sau saal tak Pyongyang ke aas-paas Chinese raaj raha. Ye pehli baar tha jab badi whale ne jheenge ko nigla.

### S10 | HOST | stickman points to a map
Lekin phir uttar se ek aisa rajya utha jise North Korea aaj bhi apna asli purvaj maanta hai.

## CHAPTER 2: Goguryeo: wo samrajya jo China se nahi dara

### S11 | MAP | Goguryeo territory grows across Manchuria and the northern peninsula; label "Goguryeo · 37 BCE (paramparagat tithi)"
Goguryeo. Paramparagat taur pe iski shuruaat 37 BCE maani jaati hai. Ye ek pahadi, ladaku rajya tha jo aaj ke Manchuria se lekar uttar Korea tak phaila.

### S12 | TEXT | stamp "313 CE · Lelang khatam"
313 CE mein Goguryeo ne Lelang pe kabza kar liya. Korean zameen se Chinese raaj khatam ho gaya.

### S13 | MAP | capital pin jumps from Ji'an/Gungnae on the Yalu to Pyongyang; label "427 CE"
427 mein Goguryeo ne apni rajdhani Pyongyang shift kar di. Pehli baar Pyongyang ek Korean samrajya ka dil bana.

### S14 | STAT-COUNTER | Chinese army counter racing to "11,33,800", then a "CLAIMED" tag appears
Phir 612 mein China ka Sui vansh aaya. Sui ke records ke mutabik is hamle mein gyarah lakh se zyada sainik the. Historians maante hain ye sankhya badha-chadha kar likhi gayi. Lekin itna saaf hai ki ye us zamane ke sabse bade hamlon mein se ek tha.

### S15 | MAP | animated river crossing at the Salsu (Chongchon); retreating arrows break apart; counter "3,05,000 → 2,700"
Goguryeo ke general Eulji Mundeok ne ek chaal chali. Unhone shanti ka natak kiya, aur jab Sui fauj wapas lauti, to Salsu nadi pe us pe toot pade. Chinese records ke mutabik teen lakh paanch hazaar sainik nadi paar aaye the. Wapas sirf lagbhag do hazaar saat sau pahunche.

### S16 | TEXT | kinetic text "Battle of Salsu, 612"; river ripples under the title
Ye hai Battle of Salsu. North Korea ke liye ye sirf ek jung nahi, ek sabak hai: chhota desh bhi duniya ki sabse badi taqat ko rok sakta hai.

### S17 | MAP | Silla (south-east) and Tang (west) arrows close in; Baekje falls "660", Goguryeo falls "668"
Lekin Goguryeo ko akhir mein ek Korean rajya ne hi giraya. Dakshin ka rajya Silla, China ke Tang vansh ke saath mil gaya. 660 mein Baekje gira. 668 mein Goguryeo.

### S18 | MAP | Unified Silla in the south, Balhae in the north; label "Balhae · 698"
Silla ne baad mein Tang ko bhi bahar nikal diya aur dakshin ko ek kiya. Uttar mein Goguryeo ke ek purane general ne naya rajya banaya: Balhae. Is pe bhi aaj China, North aur South Korea ke historians mein behes hai ki ye kiska itihaas hai.

### S19 | GRAPHIC | letters morph: "Goguryeo" → "Goryeo" → "Korea"; UNESCO-style badge "Koguryo Tombs, 2004"
Aur ek mazedaar baat: angrezi ka shabd "Korea" Goryeo se aaya, jo Goguryeo ka hi chhota roop tha. 2004 mein Goguryeo ki kabron ko UNESCO World Heritage ka darja mila. Ye North Korea ki pehli World Heritage site thi.

### S20 | HOST | stickman taps temple
Yaad rakhiye. North Korea khud ko Goguryeo ka waaris maanta hai, us rajya ka jo China ke saamne nahi jhuka. Ab dekhiye agle hazaar saal mein kya hua.
