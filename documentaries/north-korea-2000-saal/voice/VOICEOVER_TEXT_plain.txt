CH01 · CHAPTER 1: Ek bhaalu, ek devta aur pehla Chinese raaj · ~1:41 min · file name: ch01.mp3
==============================

Korea ki kahani ek myth se shuru hoti hai. Ek bhaalu gufa mein band raha, sirf lahsun aur mugwort kha kar, aur aurat ban gayi. Uski shaadi aasmaan ke devta ke bete se hui. Unka beta tha Dangun. Myth ke hisaab se Dangun ne 2333 BCE mein pehla Korean rajya banaya, Gojoseon.

Ye ek kahani hai. Lekin 1993 mein North Korea ne ailaan kiya ki use Dangun ki asli haddiyan mil gayi hain, paanch hazaar saal purani. Pyongyang ke paas ek bada mausoleum bana diya gaya. Kim Il Sung kehte the ki Dangun koi myth nahi, asli insaan the.

Bahar ke historians is daave ko nahi maante. North Korea ne kisi independent jaanch ki ijaazat nahi di. South Korean historians ke mutabik ye kabr shayad bahut baad ke Goguryeo daur ki hai. Lekin sawaal ye hai ki ek desh ko paanch hazaar saal purane devta ki zaroorat kyun padi? Is sawaal pe hum wapas aayenge.

Ab asli itihaas. 108 BCE mein China ke Han samrajya ne Gojoseon ko hara diya. Uttar Korea mein chaar Chinese prashasanik ilaake bane. Inme sabse bada tha Lelang, jiska kendra aaj ke Pyongyang ke paas tha.

Lagbhag chaar sau saal tak Pyongyang ke aas-paas Chinese raaj raha. Ye pehli baar tha jab badi whale ne jheenge ko nigla.

Lekin phir uttar se ek aisa rajya utha jise North Korea aaj bhi apna asli purvaj maanta hai.

CH02 · CHAPTER 2: Goguryeo: wo samrajya jo China se nahi dara · ~2:27 min · file name: ch02.mp3
==============================

Goguryeo. Paramparagat taur pe iski shuruaat 37 BCE maani jaati hai. Ye ek pahadi, ladaku rajya tha jo aaj ke Manchuria se lekar uttar Korea tak phaila.

313 CE mein Goguryeo ne Lelang pe kabza kar liya. Korean zameen se Chinese raaj khatam ho gaya.

427 mein Goguryeo ne apni rajdhani Pyongyang shift kar di. Pehli baar Pyongyang ek Korean samrajya ka dil bana.

Phir 612 mein China ka Sui vansh aaya. Sui ke records ke mutabik is hamle mein gyarah lakh se zyada sainik the. Historians maante hain ye sankhya badha-chadha kar likhi gayi. Lekin itna saaf hai ki ye us zamane ke sabse bade hamlon mein se ek tha.

Goguryeo ke general Eulji Mundeok ne ek chaal chali. Unhone shanti ka natak kiya, aur jab Sui fauj wapas lauti, to Salsu nadi pe us pe toot pade. Chinese records ke mutabik teen lakh paanch hazaar sainik nadi paar aaye the. Wapas sirf lagbhag do hazaar saat sau pahunche.

Ye hai Battle of Salsu. North Korea ke liye ye sirf ek jung nahi, ek sabak hai: chhota desh bhi duniya ki sabse badi taqat ko rok sakta hai.

Lekin Goguryeo ko akhir mein ek Korean rajya ne hi giraya. Dakshin ka rajya Silla, China ke Tang vansh ke saath mil gaya. 660 mein Baekje gira. 668 mein Goguryeo.

Silla ne baad mein Tang ko bhi bahar nikal diya aur dakshin ko ek kiya. Uttar mein Goguryeo ke ek purane general ne naya rajya banaya: Balhae. Is pe bhi aaj China, North aur South Korea ke historians mein behes hai ki ye kiska itihaas hai.

Aur ek mazedaar baat: angrezi ka shabd "Korea" Goryeo se aaya, jo Goguryeo ka hi chhota roop tha. 2004 mein Goguryeo ki kabron ko UNESCO World Heritage ka darja mila. Ye North Korea ki pehli World Heritage site thi.

Yaad rakhiye. North Korea khud ko Goguryeo ka waaris maanta hai, us rajya ka jo China ke saamne nahi jhuka. Ab dekhiye agle hazaar saal mein kya hua.

CH03 · CHAPTER 3: Mongol, Japan, Manchu: har taraf se whales · ~1:58 min · file name: ch03.mp3
==============================

918 mein Wang Geon ne Goryeo rajya banaya, aur 935 tak poora prayadweep ek ho gaya.

Phir aaye Mongol. 1231 se Mongol hamle shuru hue, aur dashakon ki ladai ke baad Goryeo Mongol samrajya ka adheen rajya ban gaya. Lagbhag ek sadi tak.

1392 mein General Yi Seong-gye ne naya vansh shuru kiya: Joseon. Isi Joseon ke Raja Sejong ne 1443 mein Korean lipi Hangul banayi, taaki aam log bhi padh-likh sakein. Aur North Korea aaj bhi khud ko "Chosŏn" hi kehta hai. Naam wahi purana hai.

1592 mein Japan ke Toyotomi Hideyoshi ne Korea pe hamla kiya. Japanese fauj do mahine ke andar Pyongyang tak pahunch gayi. Phir China ki Ming fauj Korea ki madad ko aayi, aur February 1593 mein Pyongyang wapas liya gaya.

Samundar mein Admiral Yi Sun-sin ke jahazon ne Japan ki supply line kaat di. Saat saal ki jung ke baad Japan laut gaya. Lekin socho, ladai Japan aur China ki thi, aur tabahi Korea ki zameen pe hui.

Yahi hai wo kahawat. Whales ladin, jheenge ki peeth tooti.

Aur turant baad uttar se Manchu aaye: 1627 mein, aur phir 1636 mein. Joseon ke raja ko Manchu shaasak ke saamne jhukna pada.

In sab hamlon ke baad Joseon ne ek faisla kiya: darwaze band. Videshiyon se door. Pashchim ne Korea ko naam diya "Hermit Kingdom", yaani sannyaasi rajya. ... Sunne mein jaana-pehchana laga? Aaj ka North Korea ye aadat apne itihaas se laaya hai.

Lekin darwaze band karne se duniya rukti nahi. Unnees-vi sadi mein duniya ne darwaza khatkhataya nahi, seedha tod diya. Aur shuruaat phir Pyongyang se hui.

CH04 · CHAPTER 4: 1866–1945: jab darwaza toda gaya · ~1:54 min · file name: ch04.mp3
==============================

1866. Ek American vyapaari jahaz, General Sherman, Taedong nadi mein Pyongyang ki taraf badha. Korean adhikaariyon ne baar-baar roka. Jahaz nahi ruka.

Jahaz ret mein phans gaya. Takraav badha. Aur 2 September 1866 ko Pyongyang ke governor ke order pe jalte hue rafts bhej kar jahaz ko aag laga di gayi. Jahaz pe koi nahi bacha.

Aaj North Korea daava karta hai ki is hamle ka netritva Kim Il Sung ke pardada ne kiya tha. Iska koi independent saboot nahi hai. Lekin aap dekh sakte hain, Kim parivaar ki kahani ko America se ladai ke itihaas mein kaise joda gaya.

Phir whales asli mein ladin. 1894 mein China aur Japan ki jung hui, aur 1904 mein Russia aur Japan ki. Dono baar ladai ka ek bada mudda tha Korea. Dono baar Japan jeeta.

1910 mein Japan ne Korea ko poori tarah apni colony bana liya. Agle paintees saal Korea pe Japan ka raaj raha. Baad ke saalon mein Korean bhasha pe rok lagi, aur logon pe Japanese naam rakhne ka dabaav daala gaya.

1 March 1919 ko poore Korea mein aazaadi ke liye pradarshan hue. Japan ne inhe sakhti se kuchal diya. Lekin aazaadi ki aag bujhi nahi.

1930 ke dashak mein Manchuria ke jungalon mein Japan ke khilaaf guerrilla lad rahe the. Inme ek naujawan tha, Kim Il Sung. Baad mein wo Soviet Red Army ki ek brigade mein shaamil hua.

1945 mein Japan haara. Korea aazaad hua. Lekin aazaadi ke saath aayi do nayi whales. Aur is baar unhone Korea ko beech se kaat diya.

CH05 · CHAPTER 5: Do Korea aur pehla Kim · ~1:26 min · file name: ch05.mp3
==============================

1945. Korea ko 38th parallel pe baant diya gaya. Upar Soviet Union, neeche America. 9 September 1948 ko uttar mein Democratic People's Republic of Korea bana, yaani North Korea. Uska leader bana 36 saal ka Kim Il Sung.

1950 mein Kim Il Sung ki fauj ne South Korea pe hamla kiya. Teen saal jung chali. America aur China dono kood pade. 27 July 1953 ko ladai ruki, lekin sirf armistice hua. Peace treaty aaj tak sign nahi hui.

Is jung mein andaazan pachchees se tees lakh log maare gaye, zyadatar aam naagrik. Pyongyang lagbhag poora tabaah ho gaya. Aur ek baar phir: whales ladin, jheenga toota.

Jung ke baad Kim Il Sung ne jo vichaardhara di, uska naam tha Juche, yaani aatmanirbharta. Do hazaar saal ke hamlon ke baad sandesh saaf tha: kisi pe bharosa mat karo. Na China pe, na Russia pe, na America pe.

Aur yahin Kim Il Sung ne wo kiya jo kisi communist desh mein nahi hua tha. Unhone tay kiya ki unke baad satta unke bete ko milegi. Ab samajh aaya ki Dangun ki zaroorat kyun thi? Ek parivaar ke raaj ko paanch hazaar saal purani pavitra kahani se jodna tha.

CH06 · CHAPTER 6: Doosra Kim: akaal aur atom bomb · ~1:11 min · file name: ch06.mp3
==============================

Kim Jong Il ki official kahani kehti hai ki unka janam 1942 mein pavitra Mount Paektu pe hua. Soviet records kehte hain: 1941, Soviet Union ka ek gaon. Historians mein is pe bhi behes hai.

1980 mein unhe duniya ke saamne waaris banaya gaya, aur chaudah saal tak taiyaar kiya gaya. 8 July 1994 ko Kim Il Sung ki maut hui, aur satta bete ko mili. Baad mein Kim Il Sung ko desh ka "Eternal President" ghoshit kar diya gaya.

Usi waqt Soviet Union toot chuka tha aur madad band ho gayi. 1990s mein bhayankar akaal pada. Maut ke andaaze 2.4 lakh se 35 lakh tak hain. Zyadatar demographers 6 se 10 lakh ke beech maante hain.

Aur Kim Jong Il ki policy thi Songun, yaani military first. 9 October 2006 ko North Korea ne apna pehla nuclear test kiya.

Ye tha jheenge ka jawab. Do hazaar saal tak whales se maar khaane ke baad, jheenge ne kaante uga liye.

CH07 · CHAPTER 7: Teesra Kim aur 2026 · ~1:55 min · file name: ch07.mp3
==============================

2011 mein Kim Jong Il ki maut hui. Unke sabse chhote bete Kim Jong Un ko sirf pandrah mahine pehle waaris dikhaya gaya tha. Umar lagbhag 27 se 29 saal.

Kursi pakki karne ke liye 2013 mein unke phoopha Jang Song Thaek ko gaddaar keh kar maut di gayi. 2017 mein unke sautele bhai Kim Jong Nam ki Malaysia ke airport pe VX nerve agent se hatya hui. Duniya ne iske peeche Pyongyang ko maana. North Korea ne inkaar kiya.

2017 tak chhe nuclear test ho chuke the. 2018 mein Kim Jong Un aur America ke President Donald Trump Singapore mein mile. Lekin koi pakki deal nahi hui.

Aur ab North Korea ne Russia ka haath thaama hai. South Korean intelligence ke mutabik 2026 ki shuruaat tak lagbhag gyarah hazaar North Korean sainik Russia ke Kursk ilaake mein the.

October 2026 mein afwah udi ki Kim Jong Un coma mein hain, ya unki maut ho gayi. Viral video asal mein 2011 ki thi. 19 October ki tasveerein jaari hui, Kim zinda the. Lekin sawaal reh gaya: Kim ke baad kaun?

Aur us tasveer mein unke saath thi unki beti, jise duniya Kim Ju Ae ke naam se jaanti hai, lagbhag terah saal ki. South Korea ki intelligence ke mutabik use waaris ke taur pe taiyaar kiya ja raha hai. North Korea ne aaj tak iska koi official ailaan nahi kiya.

Aur November 2026 mein North Korea ne naya samvidhan jaari kiya. Isme South Korea ko ek alag desh maana gaya, aur nuclear hathiyaron pe Kim ka command aur mazboot kiya gaya.

Ab poori kahani ek saath dekhiye. Han China. Sui. Mongol. Japan. Manchu. Phir Japan. Phir America aur China ki jung. Do hazaar saal, aur har sadi mein koi badi taqat is chhote se prayadweep pe chadhi.

Isliye North Korea ka jawab hai Juche, band darwaze, aur nuclear hathiyaar. Ye jawab sahi hai ya galat, iski keemat kaun chuka raha hai, ye alag behes hai. Aur iski sabse badi keemat North Korea ke apne log chuka rahe hain. Lekin is soch ki jad samajhni ho, to do hazaar saal peeche jaana padta hai.
