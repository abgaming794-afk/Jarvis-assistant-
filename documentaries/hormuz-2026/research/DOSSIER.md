## 7. Misconceptions to bust on screen
1. "Hormuz Iran ka hai" → Lanes run through **Omani** waters; strait is international (transit passage under UNCLOS — note Iran signed but never ratified UNCLOS).
2. "Hormuz band = India mein tel khatam" → India cut Hormuz dependence: ~50% → ~30% (govt) via Russia, US, Africa, Latin America. **LPG is the real weak spot (~90%).**
3. "Strait 39 km chauda hai to jahaz kahin se bhi ja sakte" → Deep-water lanes are only ~2 nm each.
4. "Ye pehli baar hua" → Tanker War 1984-88: 451 ships hit.
