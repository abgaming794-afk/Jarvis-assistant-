## CHAPTER 1 — Raja jo kabhi nahi mara

### S05 | MAP | Korean peninsula 1945, line drawn at the 38th parallel; Soviet flag north, US flag south
1945. Doosra Vishwa Yuddh khatam hota hai. Korea pe Japan ka raaj khatam hota hai, aur desh ko beech se kaat diya jaata hai — 38th parallel pe. Upar Soviet Union, neeche America.

Soviet Union ko north ke liye ek chehra chahiye tha. Unhone chuna ek 33 saal ke Korean guerrilla fighter ko, jo Soviet Red Army ki ek brigade mein kaam kar chuka tha. Naam — Kim Il Sung.

### S07 | TEXT | stamp "9 SEPTEMBER 1948 · DPRK"

### S08 | MAP | tanks arrows sweep south in 1950, push back north, stop at a jagged line; text "27 July 1953 — sirf armistice"
Do saal baad, 1950 mein, Kim ki fauj ne South Korea pe hamla kar diya. Teen saal ki jung chali. 27 July 1953 ko ladai ruk gayi, lekin sirf armistice hua — peace treaty aaj tak sign nahi hui. Matlab kagaz pe dono Korea aaj bhi jung mein hain.

### S09 | STAT-COUNTER | "25–30 lakh maut" counter; small text "zyadatar aam log · estimate"
Is jung mein andaazan pachchees se tees lakh log maare gaye. Inme zyadatar aam naagrik the.

### S10 | GRAPHIC | giant statue silhouette rising over a city; words "JUCHE = aatmanirbharta" in kinetic text
Jung ke baad Kim Il Sung ne ek vichaardhara di — Juche, yaani aatmanirbharta. Aur dheere-dheere unhone khud ko ek neta se zyada… ek devta bana diya. Har ghar mein tasveer. Har shehar mein statue. Har bachche ki kitaab mein unki kahani.

### S11 | TEXT | split card: "Communism: satta party ki" vs "North Korea: satta parivaar ki"
Aur yahin se shuru hoti hai wo cheez, jo kisi aur communist desh mein nahi hui. Communism mein satta party ki hoti hai, kisi parivaar ki nahi. Lekin Kim Il Sung ne faisla kiya — unke baad kursi unke bete ko milegi.

### S12 | HOST | stickman, hands apart like weighing two options
Lekin ek desh ko ye maanne ke liye kaise taiyaar karte ho ki raaj ab ek khaandaan ka hai? Iska jawab hai — bahut dheere. Pure chaudah saal.

### S16 | GRAPHIC | timeline bar 1980 → 1994 fills slowly, label "14 saal ki taiyaari"

### S17 | TEXT | quote card "Eternal President" — 1998 constitution

### S19 | GRAPHIC | soldier silhouette in front, citizens behind; kinetic text "SONGUN: military pehle"

### S20 | STAT-COUNTER | mountain silhouette with seismic line spike; "9 Oct 2006 — pehla nuclear test"

### S21 | HOST | stickman taps the desk

## CHAPTER 3 — Jaldi ka waaris: Kim Jong Un

### S22 | TEXT | medical-chart style card "Aug 2008 — reported stroke" with "reportedly" tag
2008 mein, reports ke mutabik, Kim Jong Il ko stroke hua. Achanak sawaal khada ho gaya — agar unhe kuch ho gaya, to agla kaun?

### S23 | GRAPHIC | family tree with three son silhouettes; first one gets an airport/passport icon and fades

### S26 | TEXT | big contrast card "Pita: 52 saal ki umar" vs "Beta: lagbhag 27–29 saal"
Pita ko satta lagbhag 52 saal ki umar mein mili thi. Kim Jong Un ko lagbhag 27 se 29 saal ki umar mein. Na fauj mein lamba anubhav, na party mein. Bahar ke experts ne kaha — ye zyada din nahi tikenge.

## CHAPTER 5 — Agla waaris: ek 13 saal ki ladki?

### S36 | FOOTAGE | AI illustration: a girl in a white coat beside a giant missile at night, holding a man's hand, both as silhouettes (label "AI-generated illustration")
18 November 2022. Ek intercontinental ballistic missile ka launch. Aur launch pad pe Kim Jong Un ke saath ek chhoti si ladki. Duniya ne pehli baar unki beti ko dekha.

### S37 | TEXT | "Ju Ae?" with a question mark; note "Naam: Dennis Rodman ke 2013 ke account se · Pyongyang ne kabhi confirm nahi kiya"

### S38 | GRAPHIC | honorific ladder climbing: "beloved" → "respected" → "great person of guidance"

### S39 | MAP | Pyongyang → Beijing line, date "2 Sep 2025"; then tank icon "Mar 2026"
September 2025 mein wo pita ke saath Beijing ki military parade mein gayi — pehli videsh yatra. March 2026 mein unhe military drill mein tank chalate dikhaya gaya.

### S40 | DOCUMENT | three intelligence-briefing cards stacked: "Feb 2026", "Apr 2026", "Sep 2026"

### S41 | TEXT | split screen "NIS kehta hai" vs "Shak kyun?"

Aur ek South Korean lawmaker ka daava hai ki Kim ka ek bada beta bhi hai — jo aaj tak kabhi saamne nahi aaya.

### S42 | GRAPHIC | second silhouette steps beside the girl: "Kim Yo Jong — behen"
Aur ek naam aur hai — Kim Yo Jong, Kim Jong Un ki chhoti behen. 2018 mein South Korea jaane wali Kim parivaar ki pehli sadasya. Kai analysts maante hain ki agar kal achanak kuch hua, to wo regent ya backup ban sakti hain.

### S43 | HOST | stickman, one finger up per generation
Ab pattern dekhiye. Kim Jong Il ko chaudah saal mile. Kim Jong Un ko pandrah mahine. Aur agar aaj kuch ho jaaye… to waaris hai lagbhag terah saal ki ek ladki.

## CHAPTER 6 — Afwah ne kya dikhaya

### S44 | TEXT | timeline: "30 Sep: NIS — health mein koi bada sanket nahi" → "mid-Oct: afwah" → "19 Oct: tasveerein"
Ab wapas October ki us afwah pe. Afwah se do hafte pehle, 30 September ko, South Korean intelligence ne kaha tha ki Kim ki sehat mein koi bada kharab sanket nahi hai — haalanki unka wazan zyada hai aur wo cigarette peete hain. Phir afwah aayi, America mein rehne wale ek Chinese dissident ki post se. Aur phir 19 October ki tasveerein.

### S45 | GRAPHIC | photo frame with a question mark: "Tasveer = zinda. Lekin sehat?"
Analysts kehte hain — tasveerein maut ke daave ko jhoothla deti hain, lekin sehat ke baare mein kuch saabit nahi karti. North Korea mein leader ki sehat ek state secret hai. Aur jab sach chhupa ho, to afwahein hi khabar ban jaati hain.

### S46 | TEXT | stamp "Nov 2026 · naya samvidhan"; points pop: "South Korea = alag desh", "reunification hata", "nuclear command Kim ke paas"
Aur November 2026 mein North Korea ne apna naya samvidhan jaari kiya. Isme South Korea ko ek alag desh maana gaya, reunification ki baat hata di gayi, aur nuclear hathiyaron pe Kim ke command ko aur mazboot kiya gaya. Matlab jo bhi agla leader hoga, wo virasat mein ek nuclear desh paayega.

### S47 | HOST | stickman, hands together, slower
To asli sawaal ye nahi ki Kim Jong Un aaj theek hain ya nahi. Asli sawaal ye hai — jis desh mein satta ek parivaar ki property hai, wahan agar achanak kursi khaali ho jaaye… to nuclear button kiske haath mein jaayega?
