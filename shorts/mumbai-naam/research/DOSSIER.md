## Careful
- Etymology: present "Mumba Aai" as the accepted origin and "Good Bay" as the popular story most scholars reject - do
  not claim the Portuguese origin is "100% proven false"; VO says "bhasha ke jaankaar ise galat maante hain".
- Portraits: Catherine of Braganza and Charles II have real contemporary portraits (Peter Lely etc., public domain) -
  use with credit if fetchable; Bahadur Shah of Gujarat has no contemporary portrait -> labelled badge "Aadhunik
  chitran / asli portrait nahi" or name card only.
- Shiv Sena: neutral, one clause ("Shiv Sena ki maang par"), no judgement. "Bombay" still used by some residents.
- Religion: Mumba Devi is a Hindu goddess of the Koli community - stated as fact, no religious framing.
