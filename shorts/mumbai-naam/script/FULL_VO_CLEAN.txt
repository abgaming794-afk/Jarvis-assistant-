Kya Bombay ka matlab tha Good Bay? Nahi! Asli jawab ek devi ke mandir mein chhupa hai.

Kabhi yahan saat dweep the. Colaba, Mahim, Worli, Parel. Aur yahan rehte the Koli machhuare.

Koliyon ki kuldevi thi Mumba Devi. Mumba yaani Maha Amba, aur Marathi mein Aai yaani maa. Mumba aur Aai, bana Mumbai.

2nd century mein Greek bhugolvetta Ptolemy ne inhe kaha Heptanesia, yaani saat dweep.

1534 mein Gujarat ke Sultan Bahadur Shah ne ye dweep Portugal ko de diye. Portuguese likhte the Bombaim.

Bom Baim yaani achhi khaadi? Ye kahani mashhoor hai, par jaankaar ise galat maante hain.

1661 mein ye dweep dahej mein chale gaye! Portugal ki Catherine ki shaadi England ke Raja Charles II se hui.

1668 mein England ne inhe East India Company ko sirf 10 pound saalana kiraye pe de diya. Naam pada Bombay.

Phir samundar bhar ke saaton dweep jode gaye, aur 1845 tak ek shehar ban gaya.

Lekin Marathi aur Gujarati mein ye Mumbai ya Mambai hi raha.

1995 mein Shiv Sena ki maang par, sarkar ne English naam bhi Mumbai kar diya.

Toh Mumbai naam ek devi se aaya, Bombay uska videshi roop hai. Aap kya bolte ho, Bombay ya Mumbai? Comment karo aur subscribe karo.
