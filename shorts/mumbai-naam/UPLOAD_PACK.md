## Description
Kya "Bombay" ka matlab "Good Bay" tha? Jaankaar kehte hain - nahi!
Mumbai naam aaya Koli machhuaron ki kuldevi Mumba Devi se: Mumba (Maha Amba) + Aai (Marathi mein maa) = Mumbai.
Kabhi yahan saat dweep the - Colaba, Old Woman's Island, Bombay, Mazagaon, Parel, Worli, Mahim. 2nd century mein Ptolemy ne inhe "Heptanesia" kaha.
1534 mein Gujarat ke Sultan Bahadur Shah ne ye dweep Portugal ko diye (Treaty of Bassein) - Portuguese likhte the "Bombaim".
1661 mein Catherine of Braganza ki shaadi Charles II se hui aur dweep dahej mein England chale gaye. 1668 mein East India Company ko sirf £10 saalana kiraye par. Angrezi naam bana "Bombay".
Samundar bhar ke saaton dweep jode gaye (Hornby Vellard 1782-84; 1838-1845 tak ek zameen). Marathi aur Gujarati bolne wale ise Mumbai / Mambai hi kehte rahe, aur 1995 mein English naam bhi Mumbai ho gaya.
