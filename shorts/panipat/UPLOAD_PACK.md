## Description
Ek hi maidan, aur teen baar India ki kismat badal gayi. Par sirf Panipat hi kyun? ⚔️

1526 → Babur vs Ibrahim Lodi (topein vs 1,000 haathi)
1556 → Akbar vs Hemu (ek teer ne yudh palat diya)
1761 → Marathe vs Ahmad Shah Abdali (18vi sadi ki sabse badi ladaaiyon mein se ek)

Jawab naksha mein hai: Kabul se Delhi ka purana raasta (Uttarapath / aaj GT Road), ek taraf Yamuna, doosri taraf Thar, beech mein sapaat zameen, aur Delhi sirf 95 km door. Tarain (1192) aur Karnal (1739) bhi isi raaste par lade gaye.
