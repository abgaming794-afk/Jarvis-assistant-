Ek hi maidan, aur teen baar India ki kismat badal gayi. Par sirf Panipat hi kyun?

21 April 1526. Babur ke paas sirf 12,000 sainik, aur 15 se 20 topein.

Saamne Ibrahim Lodi. 50 se 70 hazaar sainik, aur 1,000 haathi.

Par topon ke dhamaakon se haathi bhadak gaye. Lodi maara gaya, aur Mughal raaj shuru hua.

Sirf 30 saal baad, 1556. Hemu, jo Delhi jeet kar Vikramaditya bane the, apne haathi Hawai par lad rahe the.

Ladaai barabar thi, tabhi ek teer laga. Hemu behosh hue, aur poori fauj bikhar gayi.

Phir 14 January 1761. Marathe aur Ahmad Shah Abdali aamne saamne.

Ye athaarahvi sadi ki sabse badi ladaaiyon mein se ek thi. Sirf Maratha taraf, 30 hazaar se zyada maut.

Ab naksha dekho. Kabul se Delhi ka purana raasta, Uttarapath, yahin se guzarta tha.

Ek taraf Yamuna, doosri taraf Thar registan. Beech mein sapaat zameen, ghodon ke liye perfect.

Aur Delhi sirf 95 kilometre door. Tarain 1192 aur Karnal 1739 bhi isi raaste pe lade gaye.

Matlab, Delhi chahiye toh pehle Panipat jeeto.

Aapke hisaab se kis ladaai ne India ko sabse zyada badla? 1526, 1556 ya 1761? Comment karo, aur subscribe karo.
