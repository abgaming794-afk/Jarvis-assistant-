## Description
Duniya ke 5 mahasagar mein se sirf EK ka naam kisi desh pe hai - Indian Ocean (Hind Mahasagar)!
Baaki naam: Atlantic (Greek devta Atlas), Pacific (shaant samudra), Arctic (Bhaalu taare), Southern (dakshin).
Sanskrit mein Ratnakara (ratnon ki khaan) → Greek vyapariyon ka Erythraean Sea (Laal Samudra, Periplus, 1st sadi) → Zheng He ke liye Western Ocean, Europe ke liye Eastern Ocean → 1515 mein Latin "Oceanus Orientalis Indicus" → Indian Ocean.
Naam India pe kyun? Kyunki India ka prayadweep is samudra ke beech tak ghusa hua hai.
