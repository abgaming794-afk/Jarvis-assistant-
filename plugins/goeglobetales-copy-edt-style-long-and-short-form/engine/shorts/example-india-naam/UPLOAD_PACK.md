## Description
India ko "India" naam kisne diya? Jawab hai - Sindhu nadi!
Sanskrit "Sindhu" -> Persian "Hindu" (S ki jagah H) -> Greek "Indos" -> Latin/English "India".
Persian mein "-stan" (zameen) juda to bana "Hindustan". Aur hamara apna naam "Bharat" - Rigveda ke Bharata kabile aur Puranon ke Bharatavarsha se.
Samvidhan Article 1: "India, that is Bharat, shall be a Union of States."

Aapko kaunsa naam pasand hai - India, Bharat ya Hindustan? Comment mein batao!
