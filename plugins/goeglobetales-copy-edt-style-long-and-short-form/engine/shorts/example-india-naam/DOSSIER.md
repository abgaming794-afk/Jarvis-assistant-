## Careful
- "Hindu" here = old Persian geographic word, not the religion: say so in VO (line 5) and on screen.
- No contemporary portrait of Columbus exists; Darius only on reliefs (Persepolis/Behistun). Any picture is labelled
  "Aadhunik chitran ... asli portrait nahi" or shown as a relief with credit. Wikimedia image hosts are blocked here,
  so rulers are drawn as labelled silhouette badges unless the user attaches a licensed file.
- Achaemenid / Greek "flags" did not exist as national flags: Persia shown with a Faravahar-style symbol / Greek with
  a meander + owl symbol, both labelled "PRATIK ... (andaazan)".
- No claim about who "owns" the Indus today; neutral wording: "Ladakh aur Pakistan se hote hue".
