India ka naksha sabse bada kab tha? Jawab sunke aap chaunk jaoge.

Aaj India ka area hai lagbhag 32 lakh 87 hazaar square kilometre.

Ab chalo lagbhag 2300 saal peeche. Samrat Ashoka ka Maurya samrajya. Afghanistan se Bengal tak.

Kandahar mein aaj bhi Ashoka ka shilalekh hai, Greek aur Aramaic bhasha mein.

Size? 34 se 50 lakh square kilometre. Is par historians mein aaj bhi behes hai.

Lekin deep south, yaani Chola, Pandya aur Chera, iske bahar the.

Phir aaya 1690. Aurangzeb ka Mughal samrajya. Afghanistan se Bangladesh tak, aur neeche Deccan tak. Lagbhag 40 lakh square kilometre.

Aur sabse bada? British Raj. Aaj ka India, Pakistan, Bangladesh aur Myanmar, sab ek hi naksha. Lagbhag 50 lakh square kilometre.

Yahan tak ki Yemen ka Aden bhi 1937 tak British India ka hissa tha.

Lekin ye raaj angrezon ka tha.
