# Script framework — click confirmation · hook · FOMO · high-value lines · tight pacing · payoff · CTA

Goal: the viewer feels "I clicked the right video" in 5 seconds, can't leave because a question is always open,
learns something valuable every 10 seconds, and ends satisfied — then wants the next part.
Pace ~160–170 words/min, average sentence ~8 words, one idea per beat (2.5–5 s).

## 1. Parts and timing (10-minute video; scale for other lengths)
| part | time | job |
|---|---|---|
| **Click confirmation** | 0–5 s | Say/show exactly what the title + thumbnail promised (same number, same person, same object). No intro, no "aaj hum baat karenge". |
| **Hook** | 5–30 s | Stakes + the strange part + one big open loop the video will answer. In-scene cold open. |
| **Context line** | 30–40 s | One sentence of "where/when/who" — only what is needed to follow. |
| **Part 1 — Setup** | 10–25 % | Characters, motive, money. Rising stakes. Ends on a mini cliffhanger. |
| **Part 2 — Conflict** | 25–50 % | Obstacles, betrayals, numbers. Re-hook at the start ("lekin…"). Soft CTA allowed at ~45 % only if tied to value ("ye series aage aur gehri hogi"). |
| **Part 3 — Twist / turning point** | 50–75 % | The reveal the hook teased (partly). Real document as proof. |
| **Part 4 — Payoff** | 75–92 % | Consequences, the answer to the title stated plainly, one insight the viewer can repeat to a friend. |
| **Ending + CTA** | last 8 % | Callback to the cold open, open the loop for the next video, then the CTA in the story's voice. |

## 2. FOMO techniques (always honest — every promise must pay off in this video or the named next part)
- **Open loops**: open a question early, answer it late ("is kahani ka sabse chaunkane wala hissa aakhir me hai").
  Keep 2 loops open at all times: close one, open the next.
- **Stakes of missing out**: "jo log ye baat nahi jaante, woh is kahani ko galat samajhte hain" (only if true).
- **Countdown / scarcity of time**: dates, deadlines, "sirf 30 din bache the".
- **Hidden-in-plain-sight**: real documents, little-known facts, "ye line us charter me likhi hai".
- **Chapter cliffhangers**: every part ends on "…aur tab kuch aisa hua, jo kisi ne socha nahi tha."
- **Next-part loop**: the ending opens a bigger story → CTA.
Never: fake urgency, invented secrets, "school me nahi padhaya jata" unless verifiably true, clickbait the video doesn't deliver.

## 3. High-value vs low-value lines
Every line must do at least one job: **new fact · number · stakes · emotion · twist · proof · payoff · question that pulls**.
| low value (delete or rewrite) | high value (keep) |
|---|---|
| "Aaj hum ek interesting kahani jaanenge." | "Sirf £30,000 se shuru hui ek company… jisne poora Bharat khareed liya." |
| "Ye bahut important event tha." | "Is ek sauda ne Bengal ka khazana company ke haath me de diya." |
| repeating what the visual already says | adding what the visual cannot say (cause, consequence) |
| generic adjectives (bahut, kaafi, bada) | exact numbers, names, dates |
| background that does not affect the story | only the context needed for the next scene |
Target: ≥ 90 % high-value lines. The script-doctor agent marks each line HV/LV.

## 4. Tight pacing & engagement
- But / therefore storytelling: each beat connects with "lekin" or "isliye", never "aur phir".
- Something new every 5–10 s (fact, number, visual device, dialogue); dialogue mini-scene every 20–40 s.
- Re-hook every 60–90 s with a question or a mini open loop. Pattern interrupt every ~45 s (black card, split screen, top-down).
- Analogies for hard ideas ("utna paisa, jitna…"). Contrast (chhoti company vs duniya ka sabse ameer saltanat).
- One comment prompt per video, as a real question ("aapke hisaab se asli galti kiski thi?").

## 5. Viewer satisfaction
- The title question is answered plainly before the ending.
- Every open loop is closed (or explicitly handed to the next part).
- One "aha" insight per part; the ending gives a one-line takeaway.
- No mismatch between thumbnail/title and content.

## 6. CTA
- Never at the start. Main CTA at the end, after the payoff, framed as FOMO for the next part:
  "Iske baad jo hua, woh is kahani ka sabse andhera hissa hai. Woh agle video me — channel subscribe kar lijiye, taaki woh chhoot na jaaye."
- Optional soft CTA at ~45 % only if it adds value. One comment CTA (question). No begging.

## 7. Writing rules
- Devanagari for TTS; English words allowed (company, charter). Years and numbers in words in narration;
  on-screen labels use digits.
- Every claim has a source in research/FACTS.md; hedge estimates ("करीब", "अनुमान के मुताबिक").
- Dialogue is dramatised: never present invented lines as real quotes; no fake documents.
- Respectful to all communities and religions; no gore. India maps always full official outline (rules.md).

## 8. Beat tags (write them in SCRIPT.md)
`[CC]` click confirmation · `[HOOK]` · `[LOOP+]` opens a loop · `[LOOP-]` closes one · `[FOMO]` · `[HV]` · `[PROOF]` ·
`[TWIST]` · `[PAYOFF]` · `[CTA]` · `[DIALOGUE]`. The script-doctor agent checks the tags against the rules above.
