# Script template (documentary-toon storytelling, Hindi)

Pace: ~160–170 words/min, average sentence ~8 words, half the sentences 6 words or fewer.
Narrator (N) carries the story; characters speak short dramatised lines (~1 every 20–40 s).

## Structure
1. **Cold open in a scene (0–30 s)** — start inside a vivid moment, not with context.
   "लंदन. सितंबर, पंद्रह सौ निन्यानवे. रात के अंधेरे में…"
2. **The strange part / open loop** — "लेकिन असली बात कुछ और थी…" + a question the video will answer.
3. **Promise** — one line: what this story is and why it matters to Bharat today.
4. **Go back to the start** — chapter 1 (black chapter card). Characters, money, motive.
5. **Chapters 2–4** — each opens with a time/place caption, has one dialogue mini-scene, one real document
   (`realdoc` + source), one number moment (counter / top-down hands / chart) and ends on a mini cliffhanger.
6. **Analogy** for any hard idea (split screen: "उतना पैसा, जितना…").
7. **Ending** — callback to the cold open, one-line twist, subscribe line as part of the story.

## Writing rules
- Devanagari for TTS; English words allowed (company, charter). Years and numbers in words.
- Every claim needs a source in research/FACTS.md; hedge estimates ("करीब", "अनुमान के मुताबिक").
- Dialogue is dramatised: never present invented lines as real quotes; no fake documents.
- Respectful to all communities and religions; no gore.
- India maps always use the full official outline (see rules.md).
