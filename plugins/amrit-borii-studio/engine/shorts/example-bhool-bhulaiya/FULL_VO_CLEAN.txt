Maharashtra ke ek ghaas ke maidan mein patthar ki bhool-bhulaiya mili hai, aur aisa hi design 2400 saal purane Greek sikkon pe hai. Ye yahan kaise aaya?

Ye hai Solapur ka Boramani. 15 gol chakkar, lagbhag 50 by 50 foot. India ki sabse badi gol bhool-bhulaiya, pichhla record sirf 11 chakkar.

Ise local conservation walon ne dhoondha, aur Deccan College ke archaeologist Sachin Patil ne pehchana.

Ab chalo Greece ka Crete dweep. Wahan Knossos ke chaandi ke sikkon pe, aisi hi bhool-bhulaiya hai.

Kahani hai, ise Daedalus ne banaya tha, Minotaur ko qaid karne ke liye.

Boramani ki umar ka andaaza hai lagbhag 2000 saal, Satavahana daur. Pakki jaanch abhi baaki hai.

Usi daur mein, Strabo ke mutabik, har saal 120 jahaz Egypt se India aate the.

Satavahana raja ke sikke pe bhi jahaz bana hai.

Aur Roman shehar Pompeii mein mili haathi-daant ki ek murti, Lakshmi ya Yakshi, shayad Maharashtra ke Bhokardan mein bani.

Ek theory kehti hai, ye bhool-bhulaiyan vyapariyon ke raaste ke nishaan the, samundar kinare se Paithan aur Ter jaise bazaaron tak.

Satara, Sangli aur Kolhapur mein bhi aisi mili hain. Local log inhe kode kehte hain, aur chakravyuh bhi, Abhimanyu wala.
