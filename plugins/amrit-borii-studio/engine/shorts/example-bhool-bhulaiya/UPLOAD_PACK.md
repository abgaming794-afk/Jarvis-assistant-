## Description
Solapur (Maharashtra) ke Boramani ghaas ke maidan mein patthar ki ek gol bhool-bhulaiya mili hai: 15 chakkar,
lagbhag 50 x 50 foot. India mein ab tak ki sabse badi GOL bhool-bhulaiya. Iska design Crete (Greece) ke Knossos ke
2400 saal purane sikkon jaisa hai. Umar ka andaaza ~2000 saal (Satavahana daur), pakki jaanch abhi baaki hai.
