Duniya ke paanch mahasagar mein se sirf ek ka naam kisi desh pe hai. Pata hai kaunsa?

Indian Ocean! Duniya ka teesra sabse bada mahasagar, lagbhag 7 crore square kilometre.

Baaki naam dekho. Atlantic, Greek devta Atlas se. Pacific, yaani shaant. Arctic, aasmaan ke Bhaalu taaron se. Aur Southern, bas dakshin.

Lekin is mahasagar ka naam hamesha India nahi tha.

Sanskrit mein ise kehte the Ratnakara, yaani ratnon ki khaan.

Lagbhag 2000 saal pehle, Greek vyapariyon ki kitaab Periplus mein, ye tha Erythraean Sea, yaani Laal Samudra.

1400 ke dashak mein, China ke Zheng He ke liye ye tha Western Ocean, paschim ka samudra.

Aur Europe ke liye, bas Eastern Ocean, poorab ka samudra.

Phir 1515 mein, Latin mein likha gaya, Oceanus Orientalis Indicus. Yaani India ka poorbi samudra.

Kyun India? Naksha dekho. India ek teer ki tarah, seedha is samudra ke beech tak ghusa hua hai.

Aaj Hindi mein hum ise kehte hain, Hind Mahasagar.

Toh ek desh samudra mein itna andar tak gaya, ki poora mahasagar uske naam ho gaya. Aapko kaunsa naam pasand hai? Ratnakara, Hind Mahasagar, ya Indian Ocean? Comment karo, aur aisi kahaniyon ke liye subscribe karo.
