Angrez Kanyakumari ko Cape Comorin kyun bolte the? Jawab 2000 saal purana hai.

Kanyakumari, mainland India ka sabse dakshini sira. Yahan teen samudra milte hain, Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal aur Indian Ocean.

Naam aaya Devi Kanya Kumari se, yaani kunwari kanya. Samudra kinare aaj bhi unka mandir hai.

Lagbhag 2000 saal pehle, Greek vyapariyon ki kitaab Periplus mein, is jagah ka naam tha Comari.

Usme likha hai, log yahan nahaane aate the, kyunki ek devi yahan nahaati thi.

Phir doosri sadi mein, Greek bhugolvetta Ptolemy ne likha Komaria, ek shahar aur ek antareep.

Lagbhag 1292 mein Marco Polo yahan se guzre. Unhone likha Comari, jahan se Dhruv Tara phir se thoda dikhne lagta hai.

Europe ke naqshon mein Comari ghis-ghis ke bana Comorin. Aur angrezon ke raj mein, Cape Comorin.

Aaj official naam hai Kanniyakumari, 2016 se yahi spelling.

Lekin ek twist. India ka sabse dakshini point Kanyakumari nahi, Indira Point hai, Great Nicobar mein.
