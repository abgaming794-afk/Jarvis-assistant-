## Description
Angrez Kanyakumari ko "Cape Comorin" kyun kehte the? Jawab 2000 saal purana hai!
Naam aaya Devi Kanya Kumari se (kunwari kanya). Greek vyapariyon ki kitaab Periplus (1st sadi) mein ye tha "Comari" → Ptolemy (2nd sadi) ne likha "Komaria" → Marco Polo (c. 1292) ne "Comari" → Europe ke naqshon mein "Comorin" → angrezon ke raj mein "Cape Comorin" → aaj official spelling "Kanniyakumari" (2016 se).
Twist: India ka sabse dakshini point Kanyakumari nahi, Great Nicobar ka Indira Point hai!
