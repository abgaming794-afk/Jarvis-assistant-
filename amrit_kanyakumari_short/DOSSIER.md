## Images
- Kanyakumari temple entrance: photo Sankarrukku, 2010, released to the public domain by the author (File:Kanyakumari Temple.jpg). The Tamil board reads "... Kanniyakumari Bhagavathi Amman ...".
- Abraham Ortelius, "Erythraei sive Rubri Maris Periplus", 1597: PD (File:PeriplusAncientMap.jpg). The "Comari" label on this copy is too small to read, so the box only marks India's southern tip.
- "Tabula Asiae X" (India), Ptolemaic map, Girolamo Porro, 1598 (Magini's Geographia): PD (File:Asia X india.jpg). Shown as a Ptolemy-based map; no label is boxed.
- Marco Polo mosaic, Salviati, 1867, Palazzo Tursi, Genoa: PD (File:Marco Polo Mosaic from Palazzo Tursi.jpg). Labeled as a modern depiction; no authentic portrait exists.
- Ursa Minor from al-Sufi, Book of Fixed Stars, copy 1009-10, Bodleian Libraries MS. Marsh 144: CC BY 4.0 (credit kept). Polaris ("al-Jady") is boxed.
- James Rennell, "Hindoostan", 1782, Boston Public Library: PD (pd-old-100). The "Cape COMORIN" label is boxed.
